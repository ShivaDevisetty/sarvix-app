# Sarvix Core user guide

For members: what each part of the app does, and how to set it up.

## 1. Join

Sarvix Core is invite-only.
1. Open the sign-up link you were sent. An invite works once and expires after a while; if it has, ask for a new one.
2. Enter your name, your email and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Sign in. "Remember me" keeps you signed in on that device for 30 days.

Keep your password somewhere safe, such as a password manager: nobody can see it, and there is no reset link. If you lose it, ask the person who runs your Sarvix for help.

## 2. Connect your Google Drive

Files you upload are kept in your own Google Drive, never by Sarvix. You need to connect it once before you can upload anything.

1. Go to **Account › Personal Cloud Storage** and choose **Connect Google Drive**.
2. Sign in with Google and allow access. Sarvix asks only for the files it creates itself; it can't see anything else in your Drive.
   - Google may show a notice that the app isn't verified. That is expected for a small private app.
3. You come back to your Account page, now showing **Connected** and your Google address.

Sarvix makes a `sarvam-maya-core` folder in your Drive and keeps your uploads there.

**Disconnect** at any time from the same card. Your files stay in your Drive; the app just can't open them until you connect the same Google account again.

## 3. Spending

- **Add an expense or income** with the add button. Pick the account it was paid from, a category and the currency.
- **Scan a receipt** to fill in the amount, date and merchant. The photo is read on your device and never saved.
- **Import a statement:** drop your bank's CSV file on the page. Check the preview: money out can only go into spending categories and money in only into income ones. Nothing is saved until every row is right.
- **Budgets:** set a monthly amount for a category and watch the bar fill up.
- **Currencies:** switch between US dollars, Indian rupees or both in the page header. With both, amounts stay in their own currency.

## 4. Fitness

- **Log weight** (and body fat or resting heart rate if you have them). The chart shows your trend, a 7-day average, where it's heading over the next weeks, and your target range.
  - A weigh-in that is far off the ones around it is marked "looks off" and left out of the chart.
- **Upload a body scan (DEXA) or a blood report.** The file goes to your own Google Drive; open it any time from its entry on the timeline.
- **Diet:** start a diet period with a daily calorie target; it ends when you end it or start a new one.
- **Notes:** anything else worth remembering, on the same timeline.

## 5. Your account

- **Password:** change it under **Account › Password**. Your other devices are signed out.
- **Storage:** see whether your Google Drive is connected, and to which account.

## 6. Limits

- Up to **500 files** per member (files in the Trash don't count). At the limit, uploads stop until you delete some.
- A health document can be up to 25 MB: PDF, images, Word, Excel, CSV, text or ZIP.

## 7. Help

Open **Help** in the app to ask a question. You'll get a reply in the same place, marked as unread until you open it.
